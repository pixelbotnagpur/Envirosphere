Environmental Management Plan (EMP) is a guidance document to measure and achieve compliance with the environmental protection and mitigation requirements of a project. We can present EMP guidance documents at the project planning and approval application stage. This is important in order to inform regulatory agencies that the proponent has agreed to follow management strategies to avoid and mitigate environmental impacts during the construction and operation phase of a project.

We have completed several of these management plans, taking into consideration specific municipal, provincial, federal construction requirements and the best environmental management practices. These include a diverse range of projects, such as mining projects, various industrial projects, infrastructure projects and urban residential/commercial building developments.

Under the Forest (Conservation) Act 1980, any developmental project involving forest land requires Forest Clearance from the Ministry of Environment, Forests and Climate Change (MoEFCC). Envirosphere Consultant and Engineers has a specialised team of professionals providing consultancy services for obtaining Forest Clearance (Stage I and Stage II).

We are adept at completing procedures pertaining to Forest lands. We prepare Forest Diversion Proposal (FDP), facilitate online application to MoEFCC and interact with concerned local forest department. We also conduct Site visits, Tree enumeration, Demarcation, presentation to Committee and obtain the Final Forest Clearance from the concerned Forest Department.

We conduct Environmental Audits to evaluate the environmental compliance and management systems, do gap analysis and provide corrective actions. The primary focus is to check the effectiveness of implementing the Environmental Management System and maintenance of documents and records pertaining to it. We conduct comprehensive audits and provide detailed recommendations. During such audits, we encourage open discussion with our clients to achieve the very best outcome for them.

These environmental audits are useful tools for quantifying an organisation’s performance and position regarding the environment. Our Environmental audit reports comprise both performance evaluation and recommendations for continuous improvement.

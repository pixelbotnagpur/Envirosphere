Coastal Regulation Zone, the area between High Tide Line (HTL) and Low Tide Line (LTL) and area up to 500 meters towards landward side from HTL, is a separate realm of nature enriched with an ecosystem which is vulnerable because of the developmental activities near the shoreline. In order to nurture the coastal treasures and resources, MoEFCC has formulated CRZ notification, annexed with permissible and prohibited activities.

At Envirosphere Consultant & Engineers, we hold adequate experience in providing CRZ Clearance Services. We assist the clients to meet the needs of the project in line with the CRZ guidelines published by MoEFCC. We conduct site visits, assessment studies, documentation for the Town planning authority, the Urban Development Department and Environmental Department. We make presentations to the National/ State Coastal Zone Management Authority (NCZMA/ SCZMA) and also co-ordinate with MoEFCC recognised agencies such as IRS, CESS, NIO, NCSCM, etc.

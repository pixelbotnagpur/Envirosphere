The environmental impact assessment process is fundamental to sustainable goals development. It identifies the effects of a developmental project on the surrounding environment and the ways to mitigate any negative impacts. It also looks for various opportunities to enhance the surrounding environment wherever there is scope.

At Envirosphere Consultant & Engineers, we have extensive experience in carrying out environmental impact assessments. We collaborate closely with our clients to develop robust, sustainable and long-term solutions for the challenges they face, considering all the environmental aspects.

Envirosphere Consultant & Engineers provide Environmental and Social Impact Assessment for various sectors as listed below:

- Synthetic Organic Chemical Industries
- Pharmaceuticals Industries
- Sugar Industries
- Distilleries
- Metallurgical Industries
- Mineral Beneficiation
- Mining
- Bio-Medical Waste Treatment Facilities
- Highways
- Building Construction and Townships
- Industrial Estate/ Special Economic Zone/ IT Parks

We offer a complete, integrated service covering all phases of developments from master planning to EIA, and from strategic environmental assessment to construction environmental management plans.

We engage at the earliest stages of a project's conceptualization to ensure legal compliance and adoption of best practice standards. Our EIA studies are valuable tools for identifying potential problems during the initial planning and design process. We identify ‘show-stoppers’, cost-effective and sustainable solutions and design mitigation as projects develop.

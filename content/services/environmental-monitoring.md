Environmental Monitoring is a practice that is implemented on most of the construction and development projects to assess, monitor, and regulate the impact of development on the quality of the environment. This is an important step for preparing environmental impact assessments and many circumstances in which human activities carry a risk of harmful effects on the natural environment.

We provide environmental monitoring services regarding ambient and work-zone Air, Ground and Surface Water, STP & ETP Inlet and Outlet, DG Set, Noise, etc. for a diverse range of projects. The studies we carry out related to Environmental Monitoring are as described below:

- Developing and implementing Environmental Monitoring Plans (Performance Monitoring Plans and Environmental Effects Monitoring) & Best Management Practices (BMP)
- Carry Out Environmental Monitoring according to the monitoring plan.
- Post construction monitoring of compensation and restoration plans

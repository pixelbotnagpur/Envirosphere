Envirosphere Consultant & Engineers serves its clients around the country for obtaining Environmental Clearance (EC) from the Ministry of Environment, Forests and Climate Change (MoEFCC). At present, we serve synthetic organic chemical industries, building infrastructure projects, mining projects, bio-medical waste treatment projects and various industries.

As per the guidelines of the Ministry of Environment, Forests and Climate Change (MoEFCC), every project that has been accorded EC needs to submit the compliance of the conditions mentioned in EC Letter (Post EC Compliance). This is done by our qualified team of professionals.

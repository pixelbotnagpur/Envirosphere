Envirosphere Consultant and Engineers provide one stop solution regarding Design/Engineering, Procurement, Manufacturing, Supply, Construction, Installation and Operation and Maintenance of Water and Wastewater (Sewage/Effluent) Treatment plants and Pollution Control Equipment on a turnkey basis.

## Water & Wastewater Treatment System Design and O&M System Support

- Surface water/groundwater dewatering treatment system design and installation
- Design and installation of water treatment system
- Operation and maintenance (O&M) of treatment system
- Sample collection and analysis of system discharge water
- Permit preparation and submission
- Coordination with the local governing agency during permitting
- Ongoing system monitoring and long-term water sample collection
- Routine reporting to local governing agency

We also provide design and implementation services for UFP, RO, wastewater network and rainwater harvesting systems with the following objectives:

- Establishment of compact plants
- Easy Operation & Maintenance (O&M)
- Lesser Energy Consumption
- Less Capital Expenditure (CAPEX)
- Lesser Operation Expenditure (OPEX)
- Good aesthetics with surroundings
- Treated water as per Govt. norms for “Discharge & Reuse”
